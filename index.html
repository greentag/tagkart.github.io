About Dhami Group

Dhami Group provides sustainable and eco-friendly engineering & digital solutions aligned with modern industrial standards.

We focus on quality, safety, compliance, and long-term value delivery across industrial, ERP, cloud, and automation projects.

What We Do

Our key areas of services include:

  • Salesforce CRM – Implementation, customization, automation, user support, and process improvement.
  • SAP Business One – Implementation support, configuration, optimization, reporting, and technical-functional assistance.
  • Website Solutions – Website development, maintenance, security, performance improvement, and ongoing support.
  • Business Process Automation – Helping organizations reduce manual work and improve operational efficiency.
  • Digital & Technical Support – Practical technology solutions tailored to individual business requirements.

Our Approach

At Dhami Group, we believe technology should solve business problems rather than create unnecessary complexity.

We focus on understanding the business process first and then recommending the right technology or solution. Our approach is based on transparency, practical implementation, responsive support, and long-term value.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build Dhami Group as a trusted technology and digital solutions partner for businesses by delivering simple, reliable, and effective solutions that create measurable value.
