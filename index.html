About Dhami Group

Dhami Group provides sustainable and eco-friendly engineering & digital solutions aligned with modern industrial standards.

We focus on quality, safety, compliance, and long-term value delivery across industrial, ERP, cloud, and automation projects.

What We Do

Our key areas of services include:

  • Salesforce CRM – Implementation, customization, automation, user support, and process improvement.
  • SAP Business One – Implementation support, configuration, optimization, reporting, and technical-functional assistance.
  • Website Solutions – Website development, maintenance, security, performance improvement, and ongoing support.
  • Business Process Automation – Helping organizations reduce manual work and improve operational efficiency.
  • Digital & Technical Support – Practical technology solutions tailored to individual business requirements.

Our Approach

At Dhami Group, we believe technology should solve business problems rather than create unnecessary complexity.

We focus on understanding the business process first and then recommending the right technology or solution. Our approach is based on transparency, practical implementation, responsive support, and long-term value.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build Dhami Group as a trusted technology and digital solutions partner for businesses by delivering simple, reliable, and effective solutions that create measurable value.

Dhami Group Digital Commerce & Solutions

ERP Services (SAP B1)

We specialize in providing comprehensive SAP Business One services tailored to optimize your internal business processes.

  • System Implementation & Setup: End-to-end SAP Business One installation and seamless module configuration.
  • Financial & Accounting Optimization: Deep expertise in General Ledger (G/L) mapping, Expense Clearing accounts, and managing complex Journal Entries in multiple currencies.
  • Production & Inventory Management: Streamlining production orders, profit analysis, and fixed asset mappings for smooth business operations.
  • Ongoing Tech Support: 24/7 technical assistance, error troubleshooting, and system upgrades.

CRM Services (Salesforce)

Maximize your customer relationships and internal efficiency with our dedicated Salesforce solutions.

  • Org Management & Security: Complete Salesforce setup, user profiling, role hierarchy, and permission set management.
  • Custom Development: Building customized Lightning Web Components (LWC) and tailored internal tools (like custom Recruitment tracking applications) to match your workflow.
  • Process Automation: Designing complex workflows, custom Case escalation triggers, and automated approval processes to reduce manual effort.
  • System Maintenance: Regular health checks, API integration support, and smooth data handling.

Web Services

We create dynamic, secure, and highly interactive digital experiences for modern businesses.

  • Custom Website Development: Building responsive, highly graphical, and interactive business websites.
  • UI/UX & Animations: Creating engaging user interfaces with modern trends like glassmorphism, 3D elements, and smooth scrolling animations.
  • Hosting & Security: Secure server deployment, continuous monitoring, and SSL integration.
  • Performance Optimization: Speed tuning, backend maintenance, and bug fixing to ensure zero downtime.

Environmental Services

Aligning technological growth with ecological responsibility for a sustainable future.

  • Green Tech Consulting: Helping businesses adopt eco-friendly IT operations and digital solutions.
  • Paperless Infrastructure: Designing automated digital workflows to significantly reduce organizational carbon footprints.
  • Nature Conservation Initiatives: Supporting and integrating practical environmental awareness projects into corporate models.
  • Sustainability Audits: Assisting organizations in tracking and reporting their eco-friendly initiatives.
