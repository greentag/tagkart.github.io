About Dhami Group

Dhami Group provides sustainable and eco-friendly engineering & digital solutions aligned with modern industrial standards.

We focus on quality, safety, compliance, and long-term value delivery across industrial, ERP, cloud, and automation projects.

Our Products

Explore our next-gen digital commerce products, ERP toolkits, and sustainability automation modules designed for high performance.

Our Partners

We work closely with global tech giants including Salesforce, SAP, AWS, and modern green technology alliances to deliver reliable enterprise solutions.

ERP Services (SAP B1)

We specialize in providing comprehensive SAP Business One services tailored to optimize your internal business processes.

  • System Implementation & Setup: End-to-end installation and configuration.
  • Financial Optimization: General Ledger mapping and multi-currency journals.
  • Production & Inventory: Production order streamline and cost analysis.
  • Tech Support: Ongoing functional and technical support.

CRM Services (Salesforce)

Maximize your customer relationships and internal efficiency with our dedicated Salesforce solutions.

  • Org Setup: Profiles, roles, permission sets and security.
  • Custom Dev: Lightning Web Components (LWC) and custom objects.
  • Automation: Flow Builder workflows and approval triggers.
  • Maintenance: API sync, data governance and user training.

Web Services

We create dynamic, secure, and highly interactive digital experiences for modern businesses.

  • Web Development: Clean frontend and cloud backend architecture.
  • UI/UX Design: Responsive layouts and glassmorphic graphics.
  • Security: SSL deployment and continuous optimization.

Environmental Services

Aligning technological growth with ecological responsibility for a sustainable future.

  • Green Tech Consulting: Paperless digital workplace adoption.
  • Sustainability Audits: Emission tracking and reporting.
